We can never fully anticipate what’s to come; we don’t control the external world. The more we cling to old dogmas and certainty, the more fragile we become. To thrive in an ever-changing Universe, we need to step out of comfort and keep questioning.

By exploring the unknown and letting go of rigid expectations, we can cultivate antifragility. meue sets out to create an environment that embraces uncertainty and challenges the limits of what we know, and of what we think is possible.

meue is not a protocol, a solution or a magic formula. It’s an invitation to a shared journey, a collective exploration of life’s diverse and evolving landscape: opening up to new experiences, receiving feedback, and cultivating a movement practice grounded in curiosity and dialogue.

Life is struggle. So we keep questioning:

  • How can movement improve our lives and our relationship to the world?
  • How can practice on the mats inform how we live off them?
  • How can we become more antifragile?
  • How do we connect the individual and the collective, the inner and the outer?
  • How can we build communication and trust through non-verbal dialogue?
  • What elements and primitives are missing from our lives?

Through practice, we explore mind, body, heart and spirit. We look for tools to improve attention, partnering, coordination, rhythm and timing, strength, elasticity, endurance, balance, power, speed, patience, and much more. Each session is an opportunity to expand the horizon of what’s possible.
